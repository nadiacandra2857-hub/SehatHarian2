# Provenance ilustrasi kampus

Pengguna memilih “Buat ilustrasi kampus generik” pada 6 November 2026. Dibuat memakai tool `image_gen.imagegen` bawaan, bukan CLI/API fallback. Tidak menggunakan gambar referensi atau aset RepDB.

Hasil disalin tanpa mengubah gambar ke `Frontend2/src/main/resources/images/campus-generic.png`. Gambar diperiksa: kampus tropis generik, tanpa nama/logo, mahasiswa kecil dalam komposisi bangunan dan pepohonan. Caption landing menyatakan ilustrasi AI dan bukan identitas kampus tertentu.

Prompt final:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative illustration for the academic-project introduction of the Indonesian SehatHarian healthy-habits application. Create a polished wide landscape editorial illustration of a generic Indonesian tropical university campus, modern modest academic buildings, shaded pedestrian paths, leafy trees and a few small student figures walking. Warm welcoming daylight. Refined architectural illustration with subtle texture, emerald foliage and cyan accents to suit an Emerald–Cyan dark interface. Clearly fictional generic campus, no specific university identity. Buildings and greenery form a balanced readable wide composition. No text, no signage, no logo, no watermark, no hospital or medical branding. Opaque background.
